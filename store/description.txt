Overpass brings satellite-pass planning to Pebble Time 2, looking both ways in time. See upcoming Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-2A/B/C passes over your location with a countdown, hourly cloud forecast and coverage flags, and scroll back through the last 30 days to see which passes produced a real scene and how cloudy it was.

The regional map shows your location, the selected satellite ground track and its nominal imaging swath. The pass list puts past acquisitions above an amber NOW line and predictions below it. Predictions and their map remain available offline.

Past passes: SEEN (a real scene exists; cloud is the whole scene's cover, not the cloud over you), PROCESSING (not in the catalogue yet: Landsat takes about two weeks, Sentinel-2 about two days) or NOT ACQUIRED. Cloud forecasts more than five days out are marked with "~" because they have little skill.

UP/DOWN: select a pass (UP goes back in time)
Hold DOWN: switch map/list
SELECT: save a timestamped ground-truth pin on the phone
Hold SELECT: refresh predictions
Export pins as GeoJSON from the app settings.

Coverage is predicted, not guaranteed acquisition. Sentinel-2A uses a partial acquisition plan. The map is a regional overview and is unavailable above 80 degrees latitude. Requires phone location permission and an internet connection for fresh predictions and scene history.

Emulator-tested. Physical-watch GPS and phone export validation remain pending.

Data: CelesTrak, Element 84 Earth Search (Landsat Collection 2 L2, Sentinel-2 L2A), Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), Natural Earth (public domain). Source: https://github.com/globe-and-atlas/overpass-watch
