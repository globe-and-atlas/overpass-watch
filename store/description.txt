Overpass brings satellite-pass planning to Pebble Time 2. See upcoming Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-2A/B/C passes near your location, with a countdown, hourly cloud forecast, and coverage flags.

The regional map shows your prediction location, the selected satellite ground track, and its nominal imaging swath. Switch to the pass list to compare upcoming opportunities. Predictions and their map remain available offline.

UP/DOWN: select a pass
Hold DOWN: switch map/list
SELECT: save a timestamped ground-truth pin on the phone
Hold SELECT: refresh predictions
Export pins as GeoJSON from the app settings.

Coverage is predicted, not guaranteed acquisition. Sentinel-2A uses a partial acquisition plan. The map is a regional overview and is unavailable above 80 degrees latitude. Requires phone location permission and an internet connection for fresh predictions.

Initial public release; emulator-tested. Physical-watch GPS and phone export validation remain pending.

Data: CelesTrak, Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), Natural Earth (public domain). Source: https://github.com/globe-and-atlas/overpass-watch
