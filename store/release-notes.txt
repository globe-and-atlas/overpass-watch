0.3.0: Look back as well as ahead. UP now scrolls into the last 30 days of real Landsat and Sentinel-2 acquisitions over your location: each past pass shows whether a scene exists (with its whole-scene cloud cover), is still processing, or wasn't acquired, and the list marks NOW with an amber line. Cloud forecasts more than five days out are marked "~" as low-skill. Offline, passes whose time has gone by read "PASSED - NOT CHECKED" instead of showing a stale forecast. Pins now record whether the nearest pass was seen, missed or upcoming.
